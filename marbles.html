Marbles

Hundreds of illuminated spheres suspended at varying heights create a landscape of rolling light. The installation invites visitors to walk beneath a ceiling of glowing orbs — each one unique, each one part of a larger whole. Shown at festivals across Europe including Kronach, Zaandam, and Geneva.
