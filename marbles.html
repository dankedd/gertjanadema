02 — Marbles

A Sky
Brought to Earth

Marbles transforms the ceiling into a suspended landscape of light.

Hundreds of acrylic spheres of varying sizes hang at different heights, each one internally illuminated. Together they form a topography — a kind of hill country of glowing forms that visitors navigate below.

The title is deliberate: these are the marbles of childhood, luminous and precious and countless. But at scale, the playfulness of individual spheres gives way to something more overwhelming — a kind of sky brought down to earth, a universe compacted into a room.

Marbles has been presented at major light festivals across Europe, including Kronach Leuchtet, GLOW Eindhoven, Zaandam, Geneva, and the Academiehuis. Each installation adapts to the specific proportions and character of the venue while preserving the essential quality of immersion.
