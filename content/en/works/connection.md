This enormous, moving marble run, shaped like a lemniscate and a Möbius strip, plays with balance and demonstrates how opposites are interconnected.

The surface of a Möbius strip is given a single twist, creating a connection between the top and bottom sides. It serves as a symbol of connection and non-duality, illustrating that opposites are interconnected. A lemniscate is known as a symbol of infinity. The installation performs a seemingly impossible twisting and rocking motion to keep the glowing marble on its track, creating a dynamic interplay of geometry, balance, and movement.

The endless repetition and the passage of both sides of the Möbius strip through the sphere represent the importance of connection and the acceptance of contradictions. In doing so, the installation calls for seeking connections and countering social and political polarization.
