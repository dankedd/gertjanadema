This humming top is truly gigantic and brilliant, and the rotating mirror mosaic creates a fascinating play of reflections. The detailed mirror mosaic invites contemplation and represents inner reflections, such as individual beliefs and values. The mosaic's reflections represent external reflections, symbolizing a person's daily actions. The spinning top invites you to take the time to reflect on your own beliefs and to examine whether they align with your daily actions.

The installation is interactive with toy tops for children (and adults) to play with. The installation is called 'Fragmented Appearances' because of the different appearances of the humming top depending on the light during the day or night. The soundscape is based on manipulated sounds of humming tops and sounds of breaking and vibrating glass.

The shape of the installation is inspired by the giant humming tops of the Dutch composer Peter Schat (1935–2003), used in his composition 'To You' (1970–'72). I renovated these tops and used them for my performance 'Humming around and around...'; one of them can now be seen in the Rijksmuseum in Amsterdam.
