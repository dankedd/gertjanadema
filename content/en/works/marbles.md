This gigantic, rotating marble run, shaped like a large sphere, plays with the interplay and connection between inner growth and outer expression. The glowing marble moves between these worlds through two distinct motions: a slow, inward, and upward movement, and an expressive movement along the exterior. The rotating frame pushes the marble back up, thereby forging a connection between these realms. The varying light patterns of the LED strips represent the different processes of growth and expression, creating an enchanting interplay of geometry, movement, light, and sound.

The marble run has the shape of a sphere, like the marble itself. The luminous marble follows a spiral-shaped track along the surface of the rotating sphere. In this way the shape of the marble is repeated in the installation and the marble runs in a moving marble run: a movement within a movement and a shape within a shape is created.
