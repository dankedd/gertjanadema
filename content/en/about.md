Through my artworks, I aim to spark people’s imagination and invite them to look at things from a different perspective—to realize that they don’t have to accept everything in daily life exactly as it is, but can instead play with it—and to encourage them to reflect on how their personal beliefs relate to their everyday actions.

  
To create my kinetic light installations, I employ a variety of elements and techniques, with movement and light playing a central role. I combine, alter, and manipulate these components to forge new structures—constructions that possess unique properties and a distinct, original aesthetic. My process incorporates metalworking, mosaic techniques, 3D printing, vacuum forming, polyester lamination, stepper motors, and programmable LED strips—utilizing whatever tools and methods are required to bring the work to life.

  
For Fragmented Appearances, I created a greatly enlarged humming top and covered it in a mirror mosaic. For Fibonacci Flow, I incorporated an animation based on the principle of a zoetrope. In my marble run installations, I ensure that the tracks themselves move, rather than just the marbles. For Reflections of Hope, I selected a form designed to embody the philosophy of Active Hope, and I have organized co-creation sessions to involve people in the making process from the very beginning.

  
Beyond a sense of wonder, Fragmented Appearances focuses primarily on self-reflection; Marbles explores a process of growth and metamorphosis; Connection centers on the concept of connection; and Fibonacci Flow highlights patterns found in nature. With my new project, Reflections of Hope, I aim to allow the audience to experience the philosophy of Active Hope, empowering them to approach life with greater positivity and engagement. The slow, repetitive, and rotating movements of the installations create an atmosphere that is meditative, playful, and slightly surreal. They invite viewers to slow down in a fast-paced world—to take the time to be present and to pay close attention. By incorporating humming tops and marble runs, the installations retain a playful quality, making them accessible to everyone.
