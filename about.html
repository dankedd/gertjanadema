About the Artist

Gertjan Adema is a Dutch visual artist working at the intersection of light, material, and public space. His kinetic light installations transform urban environments into immersive experiences — inviting audiences to pause, look, and feel the invisible forces that connect people and places.

Each installation is designed to respond to its environment. Working with natural light rhythms, architectural surfaces, and the movement of passersby, Adema creates works that feel alive — shifting subtly as conditions change throughout the day and night.

His work has been shown at major light festivals and public spaces across Europe, including the Netherlands, Germany, Finland, Croatia, and the United Kingdom. He is available for commissions, residencies, and collaborative festival projects.

For curatorial inquiries, technical riders, or production specifications, please reach out directly at info@gertjanadema.nl.
