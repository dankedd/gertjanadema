About

Gertjan is a visual artist working at the intersection of material, light, and perception — exploring how form and color create meaning in physical and digital space.

Based in [Location], the practice spans installation, photography, and moving image. Each project is a meditation on presence, absence, and transformation.

Work has been shown at galleries, art fairs, and festivals internationally. Available for commissions, residencies, and collaborative projects.
