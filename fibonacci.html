Fibonacci Flow

Nature's mathematical poetry — spiraling ratios that reveal hidden order inside organic chaos. Light elements arranged according to the Fibonacci sequence create a structure that feels simultaneously inevitable and surprising, echoing the same proportions found in shells, galaxies, and the growth patterns of living things.
