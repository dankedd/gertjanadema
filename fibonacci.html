03 — Fibonacci Flow

The Hidden
Order of Growth

In Fibonacci Flow, the invisible mathematics of natural growth becomes visible as light.

The Fibonacci sequence — 1, 1, 2, 3, 5, 8, 13 — governs the spacing, timing, and intensity of hundreds of individual light elements. The result is a pulsing field of rhythmic illumination that feels simultaneously organic and precise: the same proportions found in the spiral of a nautilus shell, the arrangement of seeds in a sunflower, the branching of rivers and trees.

Fibonacci Flow asks: is beauty mathematical? Or does mathematics feel beautiful because it describes something deeper — the underlying structure of growth, of life, of time?

The installation can be configured for both indoor architectural environments and outdoor public spaces, adapting its scale to each context while preserving the integrity of its mathematical foundation.
