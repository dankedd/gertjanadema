01 — Connection

Networks of
Invisible Light

Connection investigates the invisible networks that bind us — the unseen forces of attraction, communication, and mutual recognition that define human presence in space.

Hundreds of light points are suspended in a three-dimensional grid, each one connected to its neighbours by lengths of translucent cable. As visitors move through the work, proximity sensors respond to their presence: clusters of light pulse and shift, connections brighten and dim, the constellation reforming itself around each new encounter.

The result is a living diagram of social physics — a space that becomes a portrait of whoever inhabits it. No two moments in the installation are identical. The work is different when experienced alone and when shared with strangers.

Connection has been shown across Europe at indoor and outdoor venues, scaling from intimate gallery environments to large architectural presentations. Each iteration adapts its density and configuration to the specific proportions of the host space.
