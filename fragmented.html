04 — Fragmented Appearances

Which Fragment
Do You Trust?

Fragmented Appearances begins with a simple question: when you see your own reflection, which fragment do you trust?

Mirrored panels — some flat, some angled, some curved — are suspended at varying heights throughout the installation space. Visitors find themselves surrounded by their own image, but each reflection shows a slightly different version: higher or lower, closer or further, slightly rotated, reversed.

The self, multiplied and displaced, becomes unfamiliar. The installation explores how identity is constructed from fragments — how we assemble a coherent sense of self from the incomplete, imprecise, and often contradictory images we hold of ourselves.

Fragmented Appearances has been shown at the Binnenhof in The Hague, at Hämeenlinna Light Festival in Finland, and at Jyväskylä — each time adapting its configuration to the available architecture while preserving its central question.
