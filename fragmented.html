Fragmented
Appearances

Identity seen through a broken mirror — the self refracted into fragments that never quite reassemble. Mirrored panels suspended at different angles scatter and multiply the viewer's image, questioning what it means to be seen whole. Shown at the Binnenhof, in Hämeenlinna, and Jyväskylä.
